## [employee-description]
### Diego Santos
Technical Lead • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Diego is a curious, resourceful, and up-to-date person who doesn’t feel comfortable with the old-fashioned ways of doing anything. Always eager to be an early user of new tech.
‍
He will not shy before a challenge and always seize opportunities to learn something new. That is why you will always find him happy in a diverse environment.
Backstory and Achievements
Born and raised in Brazil. He participated in planning major infrastructure works in Brazil and Trinidad and Tobago. Including one of his favourite projects, the construction of large reservoirs that solved a centenary problem of flooding in part of the city of Rio de Janeiro.
In the last ten years, he has had a diverse career working not only in infrastructure and residential but also in logistics, construction of TV sets and ERP system implementation.
Enthusiastic about creative solutions, he seeks to use his experiences in different industries combined with his knowledge of PMBOK, Lean Philosophy, Scrum Methodology and a bit of programming to see further.
He is excited to help Laminar change the construction industry.
Formal Qualifications
• Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering
• MBA in Business Administration
• Green Belt in Six Sigma
Fun Facts
• Until the last year of high school, he was determined to study history in college.
• Loves to travel, watch movies and series, and read historical fiction and fictional novels.
