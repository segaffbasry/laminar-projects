## [employee-description]
### Javier Zamorano
Senior Director • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Javier is a delivery project manager who is also a strong project planning and controls professional. This makes him particularly effective at driving projects forward using data-driven decision-making. He's all about delivery and using every tool at his disposal to make it happen.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
• Originally from Spain, Javier has spent most of his career in the UK, where he's been a construction engineer and planning manager on projects ranging from £1 million to £1 billion.
• He's previously worked for Amey and the Alstom TSO Costain JV on Crossrail.
• His career progression has been rapid because he really takes delivery personally. He usually finds himself deputised by the project manager to both plan and drive delivery.
Formal Qualifications
• Master's in of Aeronautical Engineering
• Project Management Institute (PMI)
• PMP Certified
Fun Facts
• Javier speaks English and Spanish.
• He enjoys climbing and completing triathlons as well as rock and piano music.
