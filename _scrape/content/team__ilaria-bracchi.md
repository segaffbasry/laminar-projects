## [employee-description]
### Ilaria Bracchi
Delivery Lead • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Her
Ilaria is passionate about reshaping the construction industry into one that is smarter, more efficient, and human-centered. She believes lasting change happens where people, processes, technology, and policy converge—the four pillars of any meaningful digital transformation.
‍
She thrives on simplifying complexity, turning data into actionable insights, and creating digital solutions that are intuitive, user-focused, and designed to embed smarter ways of working.
She is driven by the desire to create real impact—cutting inefficiencies, breaking down silos, and delivering better outcomes through the intelligent integration of tools, workflows, and collaboration.
At her core, Ilaria is motivated by the ambition to help shape a construction industry that is more intelligent, inclusive, and future-ready.
Backstory and Achievements
Ilaria is a civil engineer with a postgraduate master’s in information management from Milan, blending a solid foundation in construction with a passion for digital transformation.
She began her career in Qatar on high-profile infrastructure programmes, where she saw first-hand how fragmented processes and poor information management constrained performance—sparking her commitment to improve how the built environment is delivered.
Since moving to London, she has built her career at the intersection of information management and digital transformation, helping public and private sector clients harness data, technology, and collaboration to unlock value, streamline delivery, and embed smarter, more connected ways of working.
Formal Qualifications
• MSc in Civil Engineering
• Postgraduate master’s in information management
Fun Facts
• She’s happiest when she’s on the move—whether it’s a barre class, a yoga flow, or chasing down a new hiking trail.
• A self-confessed podcast addict, she always has one playing in the background, fuelling her curiosity and love of learning.
