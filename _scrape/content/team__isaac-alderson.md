### Isaac Alderson
Senior Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Isaac is driven to “doing the basics well.” Takes pride in seeing a plan being executed and delivered in a timely manner. Feels that communication and a positive work environment are integral to success. Future personal/career orientated goals:
‍
• Wants to be involved in driving the construction industry into more sustainable practices.
• Learn 4D Modelling.
• More involvement within Shape & Power BI
• Learn a 2 Language.
nd
• Run Another Marathon
Backstory and Achievements
Originally from Burnley (Lancashire), Isaac followed a thirst for travel after University. Picking up a role as a Project Coordinator in Sydney 2017. From there, Isaac was thrust into the world of Planning & Scheduling. Since then, he has worked at both Contractor & Consultancy level – involved on multiple projects across: Australia, New Zealand, UK & Canada ranging from Civils Infrastructure, Rail to Hi-Rise & Residential.
Formal Qualifications
Isaac holds a Bachelor’s Degree in English Language & Sociology graduating from the University of Sheffield in 2015. Following a spell of work & travel, he pursued a qualification in HNC Lvl 4 Construction in the Built Environment. The latest qualification was completing a CPD Certified course from the London South Bank University in Sustainable Construction & Development.
Fun Facts
• Completed Manchester Marathon. Planning Milan Half Marathon in March 25
• Padel Enthusiast
• Yomping around the world for 2 years across: Australia, New Zealand, Nepal, Philippines and Vietnam.
