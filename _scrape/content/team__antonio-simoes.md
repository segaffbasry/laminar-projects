## [employee-description]
### António Simões
Delivery Lead • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Antonio is driven by strong values and principles, respects the others and believes that mutual benefit relationships are the key to achieve long term successes. He values honest relationships, accountable and efficient teams where the team stands above all individuals, without exception.
‍
He is passionate, open minded, believing that its on the difference where teams get their strength. Antonio strongly considers that there is much to improve in construction, where standard practices need to follow other industries trends, towards a more flexible, efficient and reliable way of working, and wants to be part of this transformation.
Backstory and Achievements
Back in its 20’s, his view of a dynamic, and fast pacing modern world and future globalisation requirements, took him to an Erasmus experience in Slovenia, planting there the seed for his future international career.
After completing his Master’s degree, Antonio embraced an international Career, in Europe and Africa, acquiring valuable experiences working in completely diverse contexts as well as projects.
At 30 year old Antonio was leading a project with over 100 employees and was the company representative for all the lakes region, in Ivory Coast.
Antonio is a family orientated individual, and accepted the UK challenge to be closer to his family. In the UK, where he worked over the past 7 years, he helped delivering some of the greatest infrastructure projects in the country.
Formal Qualifications
• Master's in Civil Engineering
• APM – PMQ in Project Management
• SMSTS
• First Aider
Fun Facts
• Worked abroad most of his life, yet, loves to travel.
• Loves outdoors and being in the nature.
• Loves sports – cycling, running, swimming, and triathlons are among his favourites.
• Used to do fishing and free diving.
• Foodie, loves cooking, eating and a good bottle of wine in good company.
• Speaks Portuguese, English, and French and a tiny bit of Spanish/Italian.
• Has an adorable dog, called Maya, who is 2 year old.
• He is the proud daddy of Emma, who smiles a lot but doesn’t have any teeth yet.
