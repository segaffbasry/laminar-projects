## [employee-description]
### Claire Arbuthnot
Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Her
Claire is driven by building meaningful relationships and cultivating strong, collaborative cultures, believing these foundations are essential for delivering the best results. She values clarity and trust as the key forces that keep projects moving forward. Claire is also motivated by developing methods and approaches that enable teams to use time and resources effectively, ensuring projects are delivered efficiently, sustainably, and to a high standard.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Originally from Melbourne, Claire relocated to Germany to broaden her international experience and expand her professional perspective. She brings several years of project coordination experience in the construction industry, with strengths in stakeholder management, attention to detail, and delivering high quality outcomes. Notable career highlights include contributing to the new CSL Headquarters in Melbourne and receiving recognition for her work through nominations for Roberts Co’s Rising Star Award (2022) and the National Association of Women in Construction Young Achiever Award (2023).
Formal Qualifications
Bachelor of Design - Architecture & Construction
Fun Facts
When she’s not working, Claire is often found wandering the countryside on long hikes, painting portraits that The Louvre would return to sender, or reluctantly convincing herself to go for a run.
