## [employee-description]
### Rikki Cayanan
Multimedia Designer • Marketing
What Drives Her
Rikki is a designer driven by an deep curiosity to explore diverse creative styles and disciplines. She thrives on design challenges that inspire fresh, innovative approaches to each project. Motivated by the belief that art is a universal language, Rikki aims to create work that challenges perceptions and invites viewers to engage with the world in new ways. Her work is a constant reflection of her own journey, one that is always evolving, much like the art she creates.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Born into a family of artists, Rikki has been fascinated by art from a young age. She sees it as a powerful medium to bring ideas to life—whether through a simple sketch, a detailed illustration, or hand-drawn animation. With over six years of experience, Rikki has had the opportunity to collaborate across a wide range of industries, including medical aesthetics, insurance, telecommunications, and fintech. One of her recent accomplishments includes helping a client embrace a fresh approach to their marketing materials, creating a new branding style that not only reflects the client's vision but also showcases Rikki's expertise in design strategy and creative problem-solving.
Formal Qualifications
• Bachelor of Fine Arts Major in Advertising Arts, Cum Laude
Fun Facts
She believes that music is the perfect companion to her design process. Recently, she has been captivated by the rise of Chappell Roan, whose music has become a constant source of inspiration. She also enjoys attending local theatre productions, whether it’s a biographical hip-hop musical or a historical comedy with a pop concert twist. When she’s not immersed in the world of design, Rikki can be found spending time with her beloved cats, whose curious and playful spirits bring joy to her everyday life.
