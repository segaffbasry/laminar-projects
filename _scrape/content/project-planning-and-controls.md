## [working-together]
### Working Together
Project controls that drive delivery
Project delivery people
Our project planning and control teams have construction delivery backgrounds. They engage deeply with project teams to drive the project forward rather than simply monitor and report on them.
Accurate and automated
Sometimes spreadsheets are the right solution - but usually not. Our project control approach is highly automated which means not only can the project controls team be very lean, but they can also provide extremely accurate information and save everyone time from making reports.
Get the basics in place fast
Our processes and systems are ready to go from day one. We have internally developed and continuously improving documentation, tools and templates which take the best from all the industries we operate in and tailor them to the specific sector.
