## [employee-description]
### Evelyn Léber
Talent Acquisition Sourcer • People Operations
What Drives Her
Evelyn has an open and sociable nature and tends to create a positive environment around her. As a Talent Sourcer with experience in connecting with people, she is driven by empathy and a desire to help individuals grow professionally. An optimist at heart, her focus on potential makes her adept at understanding and supporting talent. She believes in the power of positive impact and brings an energetic approach to a team.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Evelyn's initial professional experience was in the Tourism industry, following her studies in Geography and Tourism. After a year in the field, the pandemic led to her transition into Talent Acquisition. She finds this career path rewarding, particularly enjoying connecting with people and the exposure to diverse industries. Her primary expertise in this area is Talent Sourcing, and over the years, she has developed strong skills in sourcing strategies, international recruitment, and market mapping.
Formal Qualifications
• Bachelor of Tourism Geography, Babes-Bolyai University Cluj-Napoca
Fun Facts
• Evelyn speaks both Romanian and Hungarian, being of mixed heritage.
• She's into short bursts of creativity with hobbies like painting, ballet, candle making, embroidery, or anything DIY!
• Likes staying active by hiking and wandering around the city on weekends, especially sunny Sunday walks for brunch or tea.
• Big animal lover! Evelyn has a sweet six-year-old tabby cat named Zora.
