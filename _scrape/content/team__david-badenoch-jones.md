## [employee-description]
### David Badenoch‑Jones
Associate Director • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
DBJ gets great enjoyment from being involved in the development of others and building meaningful relationships with colleagues and clients. He also thrives on complexity, enjoying the process required to fully understand complex systems, in turn enabling improved construction efficiency and quality.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Born in Australia, most of DBJ’s adult life has been spent there however, as a half Brit; a return to the motherland was inevitable. He has spent 5yrs in the UK observing the nuances between Australian, British and European construction methodologies.
DBJ wears many hats, having worked on-site for general contractors, subcontractors and more recently as a client-side project manager and planning manager. This well-rounded experience gives him a great wholistic view of construction projects.
Formal Qualifications
• Bachelor of Systems Engineering (Hons) majoring in materials and mechanical systems and renewable energy technologies.
• Cert IV in Building & Construction (NVQ3 Equivalent).
• CITB, CSCS, and SMSTS Cards.
• Australian Construction Industry White & Grey Cards.
Fun Facts
DBJ’s first car was a retired fire engine used to fight bushfires in regional Australia in the 1970’s.
