## [op6---faqs]
Frequently asked questions about Oracle Primavera Cloud
What is Oracle Primavera Cloud used for?
OPC is a cloud-based platform that brings scheduling, cost, risk, and resource management into one connected system. It’s designed for complex capital projects where visibility, collaboration, and control are critical.
How is Oracle Primavera Cloud different from Primavera P6?
P6 is typically used for detailed scheduling, whereas OPC works on a much bigger, multi-project scale, connecting the schedule to cost, risk, and resourcing across the entire project and portfolio. It supports live collaboration from different project teams, multi-user access with customisable permissions, and it's easy to use from your phone, on the go.
How can Laminar support your implementation and licensing?
We’re certified Oracle solutions engineer specialists and accredited resellers, so we can support procurement, licensing, implementation, and training to ensure effective adoption. And because we tailor the setup to how your team actually works, OPC gets used, not ignored.
