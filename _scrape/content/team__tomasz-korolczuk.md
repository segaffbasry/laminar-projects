## [employee-description]
### Tomasz Korolczuk
Senior Associate Director • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Tomasz finds motivation in his personal growth journey and in the sense of unity and shared accomplishment. Passionate about building meaningful relationships, he strives to create a work environment where everyone can flourish. Gaining a deep understanding of a topic by applying a scientific, structured approach is what really excites him. These drivers have translated into his professional career, where he's recognised for his leadership skills and his expertise in the areas of project management, project controls, planning and digital transformation.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
An accomplished graduate in Civil Engineering and Project Finance from esteemed universities in Poland and in France, Tomasz began his career as part of the project controls team on the Ohio River Bridges project in the US. He then led the planning of the Atlantic Bridge project (the third bridge over the Panama Canal) before embarking on a gap-year in South America focused on self-development, reflection and cultural exploration.‍
In 2017, Tomasz moved to London and shifted his focus to implementing tools and software for efficient project delivery. One of his most notable achievements of this time was leading a remote, multicultural team of Oracle Unifier experts that successfully delivered a company-wide Engineering Deliverables Management System for Petrofac in Dubai. He later joined th3rdcurve as head of digital in 2020, and assumed the role of delivery lead and head of consulting services at Alsvior Global in 2022. He lives in London with his wife Talia and their energetic Hungarian Vizsla doggie, Nela.
Formal Qualifications
• MSc in Structural Engineering from Warsaw University of Technology
• MSc in Civil Engineering from ENPC ParisTech
• MSc in Project Finance from ENPC ParisTech
• Certified Scrum Master
• Member of APM, PMQ qualified
Fun Facts
• Tomasz has been an avid rock climber since the age of 14.
• He introduced his wife to yoga in 2014. She became a yoga teacher since while Tomasz’s splits haven’t progressed much.
• He's a sport enthusiast who enjoys swimming, trekking, running, tennis, cycling, and yoga. He can be easily convinced to join any amateur team or a fundraiser as long as it involves socialising in the pub afterwards.
