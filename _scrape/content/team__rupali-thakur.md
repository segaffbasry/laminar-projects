## [employee-description]
### Rupali Thakur
Senior Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Her
The idea of taking the unconventional way excites Rupali that she is constantly searching a better way of doing things.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Born in a small town in Bihar(A state in India), She has travelled across the country for her education and work. Her experiences have led her to be a part of fast-track construction projects that hold strategic importance to nations, spanning sectors such as infrastructure, hospitality, and commercial mixed-use.
Primarily focused on Project Management & Cost Control, she has worked with top construction companies of India and Bahrain and has been exposed to some of the best industry practices. A curious individual who likes to implement efficient cost control principles, improve profitability and top/bottom lines deliverables.
Formal Qualifications
• PGP- Advance Construction Management
• B.E.- Civil Engineering
Fun Facts
• She is a certified fitness and nutrition consultant. Loves strength training, yoga and playing badminton.
• Keen interest in food and food photography
