## [employee-description]
### Alena Korcinova
Learning and Development Lead • People Operations
What Drives Her
Alena is driven by genuine passion for helping people maximise their potential by being their true, authentic selves. She believes that when individuals feel able to show up honestly, communicate openly, and trust one another, both people and organisations thrive. With collaboration at the heart of everything she does, she loves bringing people together and helping them work better with one another. What motivates her most are the moments when things truly shift: lightbulb moments, changes in perspective, and the cultural impact that follows. Alena values continuous learning and draws on up to date research and tools to influence and support the way she works and to find better ways of helping people and teams succeed.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
originally considered becoming a teacher, but while exploring university options, she discovered occupational psychology, a field that she very quickly became deeply passionate about. Since then, she has built a career leading and shaping learning and development across a variety of sectors, partnering with organisations to transform cultures and create workplaces where people can truly thrive.
Formal Qualifications
• MSc in Occupational Psychology
• BSc in Psychology
• Accredited in Strengthscope, Trait Emotional Intelligence, and NEO PI-R tools
• Coaching certificates
Fun Facts
• Loves exploring different cuisines, traveling, and discovering new places
• Stays active through Pilates, Body Pump, and keeping up with her super energetic son 😊
• Enjoys reading and playing piano
