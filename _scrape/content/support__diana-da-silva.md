## [employee-description]
### Diana da Silva
Fulfillment and Leadership Coach • People Operations
What Drives Her
Diana is all about meaningful connections – the kind that make life feel less like a puzzle and more like a story worth telling. Helping others navigate life’s challenges is what drives her! She believes that everyone has the potential to shine, even in the darkest moments, and being part of that transformation is what gets her out of bed every morning (well, that and coffee!). Her love for learning and working with people from all walks of life keeps her curious, inspired, and always ready for the next adventure.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Diana’s journey as a psychologist has been anything but dull. It began over a decade ago in Portugal, and since then, she has travelled through countries, cultures, and roles, collecting unforgettable experiences along the way. In 2014, she stepped out of her comfort zone and spent six transformative months in Mozambique, working on community projects that taught her resilience and highlighted the shared humanity that connects us all.
Her UK chapter followed, where Diana wore multiple hats: Counselling and Health Psychologist, Expert Witness, Trainer, and even Healthcare Assistant. She worked in fascinating, albeit challenging, environments like immigration removal centres, where she conducted psychological assessments and drafted psychological reports for court cases. On the other side of the spectrum, she supported individuals facing end-of-life conditions, helping them and their families find comfort and meaning during life’s most vulnerable moments.
These experiences have shaped Diana’s appreciation for the incredible strength of the human spirit – whether it’s in navigating adversity or embracing the beauty of life’s final chapter. What’s next for Diana? She hopes to volunteer in a refugee camp, combining her expertise in trauma-focused care with her passion for making a difference.
Formal Qualifications
• Master’s in clinical and health psychology – because learning about the human mind and behaviour is endlessly fascinating!
• Post-graduate degree in Neuropsychological Assessment and Rehabilitation – decoding the brain’s mysteries, one test at a time.
• Excellence in Report Writing – Diana has a knack for turning complex cases into reports that lawyers actually want to read!
Fun Facts
• Diana has a twin sister, and yes, they’ve definitely played the “which twin is which” game more than once.
• While living in the UK, she became that person who paints pebbles with uplifting messages and leaves them in random places – spreading smiles, one rock at a time.
• Crafts are her thing – from handmade gifts to random DIY projects, Diana loves turning a simple idea into something tangible.
• She’s passionate about travelling, immersing herself in new cultures, and exploring different cuisines.
