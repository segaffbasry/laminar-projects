## [op6---faqs]
Frequently asked questions about Deltek Acumen:
What is Deltek Acumen used for?
Acumen is a suite of tools that help you improve, test, and optimise your schedule. Fuse identifies schedule quality issues, Risk models schedule risk and uncertainty (QSRA), and 360 helps explore acceleration and recovery strategies, all in one connected system.
How does Acumen Fuse improve my schedule?
It checks for logic gaps, unrealistic durations, missing dependencies and more — helping you ensure your plan is robust and addresses problems before they cause delays. It’s a health check for your schedule that can be based on industry standards or be tailored based on your organisation’s benchmarks.
How does Laminar help with Acumen implementation?
As Deltek specialists and accredited partners, we help you set up the Acumen suite and the processes around it to match how your team really plans and delivers. This improves the quality of your schedule, leads to realistic risk models, and shows your team how to use the tools to drive better decisions.
