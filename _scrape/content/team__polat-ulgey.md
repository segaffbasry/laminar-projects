## [employee-description]
### Polat Ülgey
Principal • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Polat is a civil engineer specialized in project management with a focus on planning and cost management as well. He loves to support customers to achieve a successful project completion considering different aspects based on his previous experiences with international clients. He has high interest of how construction works together with architectural, mechanical, electrical and IT elements.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Polat is originally from Turkey. He has previously worked for international PM consultant company such as AECOM and Cushman & Wakefield for years, and he has past experiences with international contractor companies for Socar Turkey Aegean Refinery.
He also worked as an external planning consultant to create baseline project schedule for Kuwait International Airport Project. Especially in the past few years, as a project manager he has involved in many different types of projects with international clients.
Formal Qualifications
• Bachelor of Civil Engineering
• Former Member of FIDIC Capacity Building Committee
Fun Facts
• Speaks English and Turkish.
• Enthusiastic to be a part of construction projects management combined with technology.
• Enjoys traveling and fitness.
