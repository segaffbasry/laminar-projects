## [employee-description]
### Chris McGrath
Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Chris has always had a strong interest in engineering and the construction industry. Having a strong desire to continuously develop himself and help others around him by being open to new processes and developing knowledge for new more efficient softwares.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Chris started off his career as a project manager apprentice for a high voltage electrical contractor where he learnt and developed his skills and knowledge in civil and electrical engineering as well as developing the correct skills to successfully deliver a project.
After working with for smaller contractors, constructing a number of different renewable energy projects from Battery Storage sites to Gas generation and Carbon Capture projects Chris decided to take some time out to complete his Bachelors of Civil Engineering. After completing his degree Chris worked as a Quantity Surveyor to further develop his commercial knowledge.
Formal Qualifications
• Bachelor of Engineering in Civil Engineering
Fun Facts
• Love the outdoors especially walking and mountain biking.
• Lived in the original The Woolpack pub from the Emmerdale series.
• Has a cocker spaniel named Buzz who he loves taking for walks.
