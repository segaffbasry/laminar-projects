## [op6---faqs]
Frequently asked questions about Safran Risk Manager:
What is Safran Risk Manager used for?
Safran Risk Manager is an enterprise risk platform for large and complex projects. It helps teams track, assess, and escalate risks across delivery — not just during reviews — with data linked to cost, schedule, and impact.
How is Safran Risk Manager different from Safran Risk?
Safran Risk is a simulation tool for advanced quantitative analysis used by planners to model risk and uncertainty. Risk Manager is the live system used by teams to manage risks in real time — from identification to mitigation. Together, they bring strategy and day-to-day operations together.
How does Laminar support Safran Risk Manager implementation?
As experienced Safran practitioners with practical construction experience, we help configure Risk Manager to reflect how your team actually works, link it to your planning and reporting tools, and train teams to use it as part of delivery — not just reporting.
