### Working Together
Project lifecycle Commercial management focused on proactivity, prevention, and performance.
Cost certainty through cost transparency
Data driven decisions, proactive claims management, reliable cost control. It's all useless if the basics have not been done. You cannot have trustworthy costs, without integrated project controls and a thorough approach to documentation. We combine cost specialists with data engineers and dashboard wizards to bring you unrivalled confidence in the numbers.
Bringing contracting experience to clients & owners
Our people earned their stripes delivering projects on-site from the ground up, gaining a full appreciation of what is required to keep the supply chain performing and accountable. We increase certainty on cost, programme, and risk by leveraging our Contracting experience. We prioritise realism over optimism by utilizing a database of project actuals gathered first-hand.
Banana skin hunters
Our commercial team is a mix of Chartered QS’ and Civil Engineers; traditional commercial experience alongside builders positioned to challenge designs. This blend supercharges our pre-construction value-add, steering design maturity towards buildability and away from re-works, delays, disputes and cost escalation.
