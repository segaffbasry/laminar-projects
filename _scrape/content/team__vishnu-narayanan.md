## [employee-description]
### Vishnu Narayanan
Senior Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Focusing on simplifying challenges in the construction industry with technology. Data and digitalization were not so welcomed in construction sometime back, which is changing now, and Vishnu is happy to play a part in it. Working with people motivates him as great teamwork can prepare anyone to achieve the impossible.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Hailing from India, and in his four-year career, Vishnu worked as a project engineer, project controls lead, preconstruction & regional integration in US projects (with DPR USA). His work focused on Data centres, Life science & commercial jobs.
Formal Qualifications
• Master’s in Environmental Engineering
• Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering
Fun Facts
• Free time hobbies: sketching, painting both hand drawn and digital.
• Loves traveling
