## [employee-description]
### Łukasz Kozdroń
Technical Lead • Data & Digital Consulting
What Drives Him
Łukasz thrives on problem-solving and building data solutions that deliver actionable insights. With expertise in data modeling, pipelines, and Azure cloud technologies, including Databricks, Spark, and Power BI, he bridges technical solutions with impactful visual storytelling. Collaboration, teamwork, and mentoring others are central to his approach.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Previously at Accenture, Łukasz worked on a major UK insurance project, integrating a unified data model across legacy and modern systems. His toolkit includes Azure Data Factory, Microsoft Fabric, Databricks, and Agile methodologies.
His career began as a Military Aircraft Mechanic, later transitioning to roles like Demand Planner before pursuing his passion for data engineering, where he has excelled for over five years.
Formal Qualifications
• MSc & BSc in Engineering Logistics, Military Academy of Technology, Warsaw
• Microsoft Certified: Azure Data Engineer Associate (DP-203)
• Microsoft Certified: Azure Enterprise Data Analyst (DP-500)
• Microsoft Certified: Fabric Analytics Engineer (DP-600)
• Microsoft Certified: Power BI Data Analyst (PL-300)
• Databricks Associate Engineer
Fun Facts
• Renowned for his pasta and lasagna dishes.
• Traveled across Russia for the 2018 Football World Cup.
• Chess enthusiast who enjoys online play and live tournaments.
• Inspired by Marcus Aurelius: "Dig deep the water – goodness is down there."
• Outside of work, Łukasz enjoys sports, cooking, and traveling across Europe, the Middle East, and Asia.
• With technical expertise, teamwork, and a passion for growth, Łukasz delivers impactful data solutions that drive success.
