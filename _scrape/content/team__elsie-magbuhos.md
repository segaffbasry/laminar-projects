## [employee-description]
### Elsie Magbuhos
Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Her
Elsie values clarity and likes keeping things organized. She enjoys bringing structure to complex situations and turning complex details into plans that can be followed.
‍
She is driven to create meaningful impact through her work by helping deliver projects that contribute to a more sustainable world for everyone.
Backstory and Achievements
Elsie was born and raised in Batangas City, Philippines. She is a Mechanical Engineer with over 10 years of experience as a Planner and Scheduler across Oil and Gas, EPCM, Solar, and BESS, supporting projects globally in North America, Taiwan, India, and the Philippines.
Her experience spans both maintenance and project environments, from gas turbine shutdowns to the fabrication of LERS and construction of BESS projects, giving her a well-rounded perspective from day-to-day operations to large-scale project delivery. She transitioned to a remote setup in 2021, supporting utility-scale solar power plants in North America.
Through her work, she has met people from different cultures and truly values the friendships she has built along the way.
Formal Qualifications
Elsie holds a degree in Mechanical Engineering from Batangas State University and is currently working towards PMP certification.
Fun Facts
• Elsie loves traveling, and Japan is one of her favorite places that she’ll keep going back to.
• She is a big foodie and enjoys trying different cuisines.
• She enjoys space-related science fiction and inspiring real-life movies, as well as anime and Studio Ghibli films.
• She recently became a mom to her baby girl, Kylie. 💛
