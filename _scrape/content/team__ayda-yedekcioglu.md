## [employee-description]
### Ayda Yedekcioglu
Senior Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Her
Teamwork truly inspires her. She values working together to overcome challenges, believing unity is essential. Be it work or personal pursuits, Ayda believes that aiming for shared goals brings out the best in everyone. Being part of a close-knit team with clear objectives not only excites her but also makes her more determined.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Born in London and raised in Istanbul, Ayda later returned to her birthplace to pursue her master’s degree. With a career spanning around five years in the construction sector, her expertise predominantly lies in site management. Her primary focus revolved around reinforced-concrete frame high-rise buildings, further complemented by her experience working with steel structures.
Formal Qualifications
• MSc in Construction Economics and Management
• BSc in Industrial Product Design
• BSc in Architecture
• SMSTS
Fun Facts
• Speaks Turkish, English and German.
• Loves travelling and road trips, in particular.
• Enjoys swimming, art viewing and reading about self-development.
