## [employee-description]
### Jona Schmidt
Senior Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Jona is driven by a strong desire to develop both himself as well as the people around him always keen to try new approaches and ideas to improve outcomes and drive our industry forward.
‍
Having worked with many smaller contractors he is driven by the desire to make the digitisation of the construction industry accessible to all. Simplifying the platforms to make them accessible to all while retaining their function.
Backstory and Achievements
Jona is originally from Germany but moved to the UK as a teenager through this he developed a real thirst for knowledge and innovation. After school and a degree in engineering, he went on to be a Digital engineer.
In his role there he was involved in many different projects around the UK employing modern data acquisition and visualisation techniques.
He was also leading a project to create a digital twin application using Bentley Systems’ iTwin platform, managing the project from conception to delivery.
Formal Qualifications
• Bachelor's in Civil and Structural Engineering
Fun Facts
• Loves the outdoors, especially hiking in the Lake district or Yorkshire Dales.
• Plays Rugby sevens in summer and boulders throughout the year.
• Avid Foodie, will spend most Sundays in the kitchen and use any excuse for a nice meal out.
• Speaks English, German and a bit of French.
