## [employee-description]
### Alvin Purugganan
Delivery Lead • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Alvin believes that an organization is truly successful when people find value in each other in the process of learning and collaboration. Leaders leading leaders. Thinking outside the box but not neglecting to listen as there are always different ways on how to do things. The beauty of diversity in and out of construction and engineering.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
A proud Filipino with roots from Batangas in the South & La Union in the North. Graduated college in a university within the central capital of the archipelago.
With his passion in construction similar to his parents, he started working in the largest construction company (EPC & CM) in the Philippines. He had the chance to work with consultants and clients, and to oversee around 30 planners and 40 projects across different stages (preconstruction, construction and postconstruction) under Project Planning and Controls. He also had opportunities to be part of core teams in the company’s digitalization efforts & initiatives.
Formal Qualifications
• Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering
Fun Facts
• Speaks Filipino (Tagalog) & English.
• Enjoys family time, walks in the open, road trips, cook & dine & chat, coffee & tea.
• Loves to learn from the Bible and topics about Leadership.
• Fond of history & culture (bonus if he can learn the language).
