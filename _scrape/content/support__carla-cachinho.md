## [employee-description]
### Carla Cachinho
Financial Controller • Finance
What Drives Her
Carla is driven by a genuine desire to solve problems and support those around her. She believes in doing what’s right, even when it’s not the easiest path. Curious by nature, she’s always looking for something new to learn or try. Grounded and thoughtful, she brings determination to everything she does, finding joy in both the little wins and the big adventures.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Born and raised in Portugal, Carla found her way into the finance world by chance and quickly discovered a passion for it. She has since built her career in accounting, working primarily with tech companies.
Formal Qualifications
• MSc in Accounting and Finance
• BSc in International Trade
Fun Facts
Carla’s into CrossFit and boxing as she loves staying active. Always up for trying new recipes, especially healthy snacks, but she also has a soft spot for desserts (her brownies are a hit!).
