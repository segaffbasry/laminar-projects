## [employee-description]
### Michael Chen
Delivery Lead • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Michael thrives on human connections and values the importance of establishing strong relationships. As a result, he enjoys collaborating with teammates and clients to develop innovative solutions for complex challenges. He believes in fostering positive interactions in the workplace and empowering the people around him. Committed to excellence, he will go above and beyond to help his team deliver the best possible outcomes.
‍
Michael is also passionate about continuous learning and development, both professionally and personally. With a keen interest in tech, he believes in leveraging new and existing tools to enhance productivity and drive better results.
Backstory and Achievements
Michael is originally from Melbourne, Australia - where he completed his studies and began his career in a global engineering consultancy. During this time he was able to work with private and public sector clients on large infrastructure projects such as the Suburban Rail Loop and the Melbourne Metro.
Seeking new challenges, he decided to move to London and pivot his career into Project Controls. As a consultant, Michael has worked for tier-one contractors and government authorities providing planning and cost controls services with a strong focus on business intelligence reporting and data analytics.
Formal Qualifications
• Bachelor’s Degree in Civil Engineering
Fun Facts
• Big foodie, always keen to try new places and new veggie recipes.
• Can speak English, Mandarin, Taiwanese, and currently learning Romanian.
• Enjoys travelling, staying active, and Guinness tasting sessions with friends.
