## [employee-description]
### Sam Donnelly
Senior Principal • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Sam likes bringing calm, clarity and confidence to complex infrastructure programmes — especially when things start to feel a bit tangled. He’s motivated by helping teams make good, well-informed decisions through clear, evidence-led planning and practical project controls, and isn’t afraid to challenge optimism bias when plans look a little too optimistic.
‍
He’s a big believer in integrated project controls, breaking down silos, and making sure people actually talk to each other. What he enjoys most is building strong, high-performing teams and leaving behind better capability than he found.
Backstory and Achievements
Based in the UK, Sam has around 20 years’ experience across highways, rail and underground infrastructure. He’s worked his way from assistant project manager through to senior leadership roles covering programme, portfolio and assurance. Over the years he’s led planning and integrated project controls on multi-billion-pound programmes, helped embed best practice, strengthened governance, and been part of teams delivering award-winning project controls outcomes.
Formal Qualifications
• Bachelor’s Honours Degree in Business Administration
• Member of the Association of Project Management (MAPM)
• Member of the Project Management Institute (PMI)
• Member of the Chartered Institution of Highways & Transportation (MCHIT)
• Member of the Controls & Skills Authority (MCaSA)
• Project Management Professional (PMP)
• Practitioner – Project Planning & Control™ (PPC)
• CSCS – Professionally Qualified Person
Fun Facts
• Enjoys DIY and building projects (sometimes bigger than first planned)
• Loves cooking, BBQ’ing and growing his own vegetables
• Keeps a small number of pigs and cows
• Enjoyed playing rugby for 18 years and now purely an armchair supporter
• Earned a Blue Peter badge for singing at the age of 10
