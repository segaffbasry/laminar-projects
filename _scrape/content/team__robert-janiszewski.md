## [employee-description]
### Robert Janiszewski
Support Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Robert has always been intrigued to learn, his grandfather use to tell him that “ The day you stop learning, is the day you die” bit extreme but he really likes this. Robert Believes that work gives also a routine and structure that enables the best performance for him, this inner drive to goals and outcomes is something he has seeked in all aspects of his life. He also believes that team work makes the dream work and loves meeting and working with people from all walks of life, something that really drew him to Laminar. Robert is excited to join an ever expanding company and hopes to one day lead Laminar projects in a new country within the world.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Originally from Australia, Robert has a background in trade and construction management work. He has been shaped by experiences using his hands on different aspects of building things, from Roofs in Italy, to tessellated floors in the government building of Melbourne to even creating a life size dinosaur head for an art exhibition.
Robert has also delivered multiple supermarkets for some of the biggest supermarket clients in Australia and also lead his own project at 23 years young with a great green space initiative. Roberts core strength comes from a delivery background and a love to enjoy every experience the construction industry gives him in any country.
Formal Qualifications
• Finished a degree in Construction management with honors from Royal Melbourne institute of technology.
• Undertaken recently a certificate course in modern methods of construction from Melbourne Polytechnic.
• I have the Australian standard construction safety course also.
• I also have first aid certification and a level 3/4 Whitewater instructor and rescue certification.
Fun Facts
• Robert is ranked 18th in the World in his Sport Wildwater canoeing.
• He has travelled to 39 countries at the age of 25.
• He is a big history nerd, anything Nerdy send it his way!
• Robert has actually started the biggest known trail Hike in Australia, Completing 156km of the 1300km trail (One day he wants to finish it)
