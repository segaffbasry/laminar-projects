## [employee-description]
### Martin Corpuz
Senior Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Embracing the Socratic philosophy that 'true knowledge exists in knowing you know nothing,' Martin is dedicated to continuous learning within the construction sector. He thrives on solving complex challenges and is known for his collaborative spirit, consistently sharing his expertise with colleagues and peers.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Based in the Philippines, this engineer brings eight years of construction industry experience, specializing for the past six years as a Scheduler and Planner.
He is an expert in Primavera P6, with a proven track record in baseline preparation, progress updating, and detailed schedule analysis for Extension of Time (EOT) claims. His diverse portfolio spans multiple sectors, including earthworks, land development, and telecommunications projects.
Formal Qualifications
Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering
