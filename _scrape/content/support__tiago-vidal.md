## [employee-description]
### Tiago Vidal
Finance Manager • Finance
What Drives Him
He wants to leave his mark. It may not be big enough to impact humankind, but he would be satisfied if he positively impacted his community, family, or organization.‍
This goal led him to his work career, where he prioritized working for companies focused on improving the world.‍
He firmly believes in data and data-driven approaches, enabling individuals and organizations to make better, more informed decisions.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Portuguese-born-and-raised Tiago lived most of his time in Portugal with a small 5-year experience in Malta.‍
He worked in the renewable energy sector, performing multiple finance activities, like budgeting, reporting or cost management. He also helped raise debt and equity capital to fund operations and expand the portfolio of solar farms.‍
He previously worked for multiple companies within the Infraventus Group.
Formal Qualifications
• International Certificate in Wealth and Investment Management (CISI)
• Bachelor’s Degree in Business Administration
Fun Facts
• As a classic Portuguese, he loves codfish and a good “Pastel de Nata”.
• Avid padel player and “peak bagger.”
• Loves investing, currently a popular investor on Etoro.
• Trying to become a Pistachio entrepreneur in Alentejo, Portugal.
