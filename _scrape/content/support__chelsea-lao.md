### Chelsea Lao
Talent Acquisition Sourcer • People Operations
What Drives Her
Chelsea is driven by her deep curiosity about the talent market. She is passionate about understanding how different roles function across markets, using data and insights to understand each locale. Chelsea thrives in collaborative environments, partnering closely with hiring teams to co-create and advise on sourcing strategies.

Backstory and Achievements
Chelsea’s background in talent acquisition is grounded by strategic sourcing, and market mapping across diverse industries such as tech, business operations, tech and engineering and consulting.
