## [working-together]
### Working Together
Fast Delivery, High Certainty
With many interconnected systems, data centres are prone to delay as one missing piece can hold up the commissioning and, therefore, commencement of operations. Our detailed project controls ensure nothing is overlooked and problems are seen and mitigated quickly.
Adapt to Change
Change is inevitable in data centre construction, and poor management of it is the main reason for project delays. We ensure everyone understands what the changes are and their implications to ensure rapid decision-making and adjustments to the plan.
Dynamic & Effective Teams
Data centre project cultures are known for becoming highly adversarial due to the pressure to build fast and manage changes. Our people are selected for being both dynamic and kind. They push our culture of compassionate candour down the supply chain without compromise.
