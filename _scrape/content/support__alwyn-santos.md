## [employee-description]
### Alwyn Santos
Talent Acquisition Specialist • People Operations
What Drives Him
Alwyn is driven by the opportunity to help people find a place where they can truly flourish. Seeing the individuals he hires grow, thrive, and do their best work within an organization that values them is one of the most rewarding parts of his role in Talent Acquisition.
‍
Alwyn is equally passionate about continuous learning and growth. He believes that staying curious and committed to self-development not only strengthens him professionally but also enables him to better support others in reaching their potential.
At the heart of everything is his family. As a family-oriented person, he values meaningful relationships and cherishes time spent with loved ones. That balance fuels the purpose and energy he brings to his work every day.
Backstory and Achievements
Alwyn’s background in Talent Acquisition is grounded in strategic sourcing and building strong talent pipelines. With experience spanning both in-house and agency recruitment, he brings a well-rounded perspective to identifying and attracting top talent.
He has supported hiring across multiple industries and functional domains, including IT and Engineering, People, Finance, Sales and Customer Success, and Marketing. His expertise covers end-to-end recruitment— ensuring a seamless and thoughtful hiring experience for both candidates and stakeholders.
Formal Qualifications
Bachelor of Science in Business Administration, Major in Human Resource Management
Fun Facts
An athletic foodie at heart, Alwyn enjoys staying active just as much as he enjoys exploring new restaurants and coffee shops. He’s always on the lookout for his next great meal — especially if there’s a good burger involved.
