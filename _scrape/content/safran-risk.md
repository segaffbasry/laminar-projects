## [op6---faqs]
Frequently asked questions about Safran Risk:
What is Safran Risk used for?
Safran Risk is specialist software for analysing how risk and uncertainty could affect your schedule and budget. It helps project teams simulate risks, forecast impacts, and build more resilient plans.
How is Safran Risk different from other risk software?
It’s not just a register. Safran links directly to your live schedule, supports Monte Carlo simulations, and integrates qualitative and quantitative risk into one place. That means you can move from “what if” to “here’s what to do.”
How does Laminar support Safran Risk setup and training?
As certified Safran practitioners with real, hands-on delivery experience, we guide you through setup, configuration, training, and adoption — making sure the tool supports your actual workflows, not just a textbook process.
