## [employee-description]
### Vasco Ruivo
Film Maker • Marketing
What Drives Him
Vasco’s passionate about telling stories. He thrives to find the deep beauty and truth of all things, and create moving, staggering pieces through the magical craft of film.
‍
Portraying that essence in the best possible way, bringing it to a new life under his own perception, with honesty and passion, is what drives him.
Backstory and Achievements
He has directed TV ads, web spots and recruitment videos with high visibility for big, international and national companies, in productions ranging from low budget, pro bono work for solidarity causes, to high cost, hundreds of thousands of euros campaigns.
He has directed as well music videos for mainstream national artists, summing up millions of views on YouTube, and short films that got selected, nominated and awarded in various international and national film festivals.
Formal Qualifications
• Bachelor’s degree in Cinema, Video and Multimedia Communication
Fun Facts
• He’s a street photographer.
• Musician since he’s 9 (Vasco has toured all over the country playing for thousands of people, and recorded with fantastic mainstream artists).
• Addicted traveller (over 30 countries, 3 Interrails, multiple times in India, and countless other adventures).
