## [employee-description]
### Riccardo Giardini
Senior Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Riccardo believes that strong teamwork, clear planning, and a shared focus on objectives are key to delivering successful projects. He values collaboration and mutual support, seeing them as essential in reaching shared goals.
‍
He brings a calm and analytical approach to his work. He enjoys planning and exploring different scenarios, often treating it as a way to anticipate what lies ahead.
Backstory and Achievements
Riccardo was born in Bologna, Italy, and has built his professional experience across Italy and Belgium in international environments. He speaks fluent Italian and English, with a strong working knowledge of French.
He has worked in the maritime and road infrastructure sectors, contributing to projects that require close coordination, structured planning, and clear decision making. His experience has shaped his ability to stay focused under pressure, break down complex objectives into manageable monthly, weekly, and daily goals, and consistently deliver results.
Formal Qualifications
Riccardo is known for maintaining composure in complex situations and making decisions with an analytical, objective driven mindset. He approaches challenges by clearly defining the end goal, then translating it into practical and achievable steps.
Fun Facts
Outside of work, Riccardo enjoys CrossFit, skiing, and cycling. These reflect his appreciation for discipline, endurance, and balance.
