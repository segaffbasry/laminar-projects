## [employee-description]
### Athira Surendran
Delivery Lead • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Her
Athira is good at time management and determined to achieve tasks and goals ahead of their deadlines. She has a passion towards learning new things and exploring their applications in the industry.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Athira originally hails from a beautiful state named Kerala in India. She is a highly knowledgeable project planning professional with proven experience delivering large highway and construction projects in India and in the UK. She brings with her four years’ experience working as a project planning professional with Larsen & Toubro Ltd, a multinational construction contractor based in India. She has a proven track record of leading project control teams and is confident working across the full project life cycle to deliver projects on time and on budget.
In late 2021, Athira accompanied her partner to the UK. She continued her career journey as a project control consultant in London. During this period, she was privileged to be a part of the Superbloom project at the Tower of London to celebrate the Queen’s platinum jubilee.
Formal Qualifications
• Master’s in Construction Management
• Bachelor’s in Civil Engineering
Fun Facts
• Athira enjoys music (including singing, when she's alone), travelling, and cooking.
• She's obsessed with keeping things clean and organised.
