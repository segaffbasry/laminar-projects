## [employee-description]
### Tushar Patil
Senior Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Tushar is primarily driven by a deep-seated commitment to excellence. He takes pride in optimizing processes, streamlining workflows, and efficiently managing resources to ensure that projects meet and surpass expectations. This commitment to excellence extends to his dedication to sustainable practices, contributing to environmentally responsible construction projects. Tushar is keen to bridge the gap between digitalization and sustainability in construction practices. He also has a fervent interest in the digitization of the construction industry, constantly seeking innovative technologies and solutions to drive efficiency and productivity. His dynamic nature and ability to thrive in challenging work environments make him a perfect fit for pushing boundaries and achieving remarkable results.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Tushar has worked as a design and estimating engineer in UK residential and commercial projects with a focus on foundation engineering and piling. This diverse experience has not only solidified his expertise in construction management but also strengthened his technical acumen. His hands-on involvement in the technical aspects of construction equips him with a well-rounded skill set.‍
Tushar’s impressive track record in the construction industry extends beyond the UK. He has also contributed to fast-track projects in Dubai and successfully managed large-scale commercial projects valued at more than £200 million. His experience in these fast-paced environments, which demand exceptional efficiency and adaptability, has honed his skills in handling high-stakes and demanding ventures.‍
This practical experience is complemented by his academic background. Tushar holds a master’s degree in construction management from the University of Leeds, which has enriched his understanding of construction processes and project management principles. His technical prowess and meticulous attention to detail enable him to anticipate and overcome potential roadblocks, ensuring seamless project execution and on-time delivery.
Formal Qualifications
(Project Management Professional)
• PMP
• Tushar holds a master’s degree in International Construction Management and Engineering from the University of Leeds. As part of his academic journey, he conducted an in-depth dissertation study on the topic of “UK’s Higher Education Institutes Sustainability Strategy Formulation and Implementation: Drivers and Barriers.”
• Site Management Safety Training Scheme Certificate
• Construction Skills Certification Scheme (CSCS) card
• Bachelor's in civil engineering
• LEED (Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design) Green Associate
Fun Facts
Tushar is a true sports enthusiast, with a passion for playing Tennis, Football, Cricket, Chess, Carrom, Volleyball, Badminton and Table tennis. Notably, he has set a unique resolution to learn new sports every year. In addition to his love for sports, Tushar has a culinary side, enjoying cooking as a hobby.
Furthermore, Tushar’s intellectual curiosity extends to history, with a particular fascination for the Roman Empire and World War II. His keen interest in these historical periods showcases his desire to understand the past and its impact on the present
