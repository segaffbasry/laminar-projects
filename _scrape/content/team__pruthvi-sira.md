## [employee-description]
### Pruthvi Sira
Lead Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Pruthvi believes strongly in the sheer potential of the construction sector to make a difference in society and the lives of ordinary people daily.He values every opportunity equally and wants to work towards creating a self-sustainable and futuristic ecosystem.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Pruthvi comes from a small town in the state of Karnataka, India. His professional experience has been vastly around the Middle East’s commercial, industrial, power plant and Oil & Gas projects.‍
He focuses on post-contractual planning schemes, project controls, change management, delays and claims, and stakeholder support.‍
He has worked extensively on Covid-19 related delays and has successfully sought relief from critical liquidated damages.‍
Also very keen on managing resource usage, minimizing idle hours and controlling the performance indices throughout the lifecycle of projects.
Formal Qualifications
• Masters in Contracts, Claims and Delay Management (CCDM)
• Post Graduate in Advanced Construction Management (PGP – ACM)
• Graduate Civil Engineer (B.E – Civil)
Fun Facts
• A Go-Kart racer by passion and always enjoys road trips!! He has made long road trips with over 4000 Km distance covered in 11 days!
• Loves to create products (handcrafts), mostly working with soft wood and earthen pots!
• An outdoor person who loves the sun! You name an activity/sport, and he’s there!
