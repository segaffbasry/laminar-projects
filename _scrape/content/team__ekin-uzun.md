## [employee-description]
### Ekin Uzun
Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Ekin is deeply motivated by the transformative potential of AI and data science. He thrives on the opportunity to not only solve complex problems but also contribute to the future of technology. The rapid advancements in AI and machine learning, and their potential to revolutionize industries, inspire him to stay at the forefront of innovation. He’s always excited by what the future holds and is eager to be part of shaping it.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Ekin is a versatile data analyst with a strong foundation in leveraging data to drive business outcomes. He developed an expertise in analysing, modeling, and visualising complex datasets using cutting-edge techniques. Before transitioning fully into data science, Ekin worked as an analyst 2 years in Istanbul, turning raw data into meaningful insights that solve complex problems and create value for organizations. With proficiency in tools like Python, R, SQL and Tableau. He is excited to continue using data science to unlock potential and contribute to ambitious projects.
Formal Qualifications
Ekin holds a bachelor’s degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Bahçeşehir University and a master’s degree in Data Science from Kingston University. His educational background equips him with a strong foundation in data analytics, AI, and machine learning, positioning him to contribute meaningfully to cutting-edge projects in the tech world.
Fun Facts
When he’s not exploring the latest developments in AI, Ekin enjoys staying active through sports – football, basketball, and tennis being his favourites. He also loves to travel, particularly solo adventures around Europe, where he explores new cultures and enjoys sampling local beers. A lifelong learner, Ekin is also fascinated by futurism and the intersection of technology with society, and he’s an avid reader of Middle Eastern and European history.
Ekin is excited to collaborate with his new team and looks forward to contributing to projects that push the boundaries of what’s possible with AI and data science.
