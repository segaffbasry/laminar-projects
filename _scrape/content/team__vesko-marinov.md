## [employee-description]
### Vesko Marinov
Senior Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Driven by a passion for efficient and innovative solutions, Vesko thrives in exciting environments. His unique blend of project management expertise and a creative mindset, honed in the music industry, empowers him to tackle complex challenges with a fresh perspective. He thrives in transforming ideas into out-of-the-box type solutions while taking his team on the exciting journey.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Born and raised in Bulgaria, Vesko left his home country to study and graduate Architectural Technologies and Construction Management in Denmark. Being passionate about cultures, he also spent a semester in Malta as an exchange student. He was a part of several sport and artistic communities aside from his studies which gave him a strong foundation to effectively communicate and develop his leadership practices.
Shortly after graduating, he began his management career in London where he earned the opportunity to lead some great high-profile projects in infrastructure and aviation, this cemented his professional and interpersonal skills.
A while later he had the opportunity to follow a dream of his to build a career as a music producer. Within a few years, Vesko grew to be signed by several global record labels and perform at world renowned venues and events.
Continuing to expand and build on his professional skills, Vesko is now embracing the exciting opportunity to grow further with his new Laminar friends.

Fun Facts
• Vesko loves a good party and he would often be caught performing in the DJ booth in places like Ministry of Sound, Egg London, and many others.
• He is a passionate extreme bike sports lover.
• He also has the cutest white bunny.
