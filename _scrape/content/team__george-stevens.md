## [employee-description]
### George Stevens
Associate Director • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Forming unlikely relationships between clients by bringing people together to make project progress more efficiently. George finds that one of the most significant issues on projects is communication and conflict between clients causing unnecessary delays, so he uses his collaborative and communicative skills to overcome this.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
As a trained accountant, George wanted more of a challenge out of his career, leading him to join Crossrail. Although this was a complete career change, he has never looked back. Working on the biggest tunnelling project in Europe, he gained invaluable work and life skills which have helped mould him into the determined, ambitious and driven person he is today.
Formal Qualifications
• Personal Track Safety (PTS)
• Personal Railway Safety (PRS)
• Site Supervisors Safety Training Scheme (SSSTS)
• CPCS Diesel Locomotive
• CSCS Health and Safety
Fun Facts
• Travelled the world for a year.
• Property renovation and avid DIY’er.
• Holds a British Publican License.
• Can drive Diesel powered tunnel Locomotives.
• Plays football every week.
