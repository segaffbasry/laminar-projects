## [employee-description]
### Nuno Dias
Senior Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Nuno is driven by the power of collective success and is at his best when integrated into the heart of a team. He thrives on collaborating on complex projects where he can leverage his expertise to support his colleagues, fostering an environment where everyone feels backed and motivated. His primary focus is to empower his team, bridging gaps between departments and uniting diverse groups into a single, cohesive force.
‍
He is passionate about refine processes and optimize workflows, ensuring the team operates at its highest potential. Nuno takes great pride in acting as a mentor for young engineers.
Backstory and Achievements
Originally from Portugal, Nuno discovered his passion for construction while working for his uncle’s construction company during summer breaks. He has worked and lived in Portugal, Romania, Algeria, UK and now in Spain for both main contractors and subcontractors. His portfolio spans from major oil and gas refineries to large-scale civil infrastructure. He worked for organisations such as Vinci, Ferrovial and Laing O’Rourke and Technip.
Nuno has experience across the project lifecycle, operating in design, engineering, planning, QA/QC, and project management roles. A defining achievement of his career is his ability to build and maintain long-lasting, trust-based relationships with stakeholders and clients in every project and country he has worked in.
Formal Qualifications
• Master’s in civil engineering
• SMSTS
• CSCS Health and Safety
Fun Facts
Nuno is a dedicated DIY enthusiast who loves the hands-on challenge of house renovation projects.
An avid traveler who took a year-long sabbatical to explore Africa, Australia, and Southeast Asia, including six weeks of voluntary school renovation work in Mozambique.
His first-ever car was a 1974 blue Mercedes.
