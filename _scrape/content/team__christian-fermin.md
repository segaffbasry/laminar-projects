## [employee-description]
### Christian Fermin
Technical Lead • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Christian enjoys the challenge of interpreting and analyzing data, developing graphs and diagrams, and determining conclusions and potential alternatives. He hopes to build a construction system that can optimize the cost and resources through the use of modern technology.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Originally from Philippines, he has lived and worked in Saudi Arabia, Singapore, and Australia,
He has worked as Project Control Specialist mostly in Oil and Gas Projects from Proposal to Engineering, Procurement, Construction and Pre-Commissioning stage.
He has previously worked with Shaw Stone & Webster and CB&I (now McDermott).
Formal Qualifications
• Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering
• Member of Philippine Institute of Civil Engineer
Fun Facts
• Enjoy sports especially Basketball and Table Tennis.
• Passionate about Architectural Structure, Calligraphy and Arts.
• Loves to cook and to eat anything with cheese.
