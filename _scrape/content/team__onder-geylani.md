## [employee-description]
### Önder Geylani
Technical Lead • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Onder is driven by solving challenging problems with his team. He enjoys empowering younger colleagues by helping them. He thinks that the construction industry has a long way to go in terms of efficiency, automation and keeping up with new technologies. Therefore, he believes that transformation is inevitable in every field and especially in mentality.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
He started his career in the construction industry in Turkey, where he was born and raised. With more than 15 years experience in both field and office work, he specialized in planning and budgeting. Throughout his career, he worked as a contractor, subcontractor, project management consultant and employer in the companies he worked for, and experienced the different perspectives of all stakeholders.
Formal Qualifications
• Masters of IT Based Construction Management
• Bachelor of Civil Engineering
• Bachelor of Economics
• PMP Certified
Fun Facts
• Enjoys travelling, reading books, playing chess and other games of strategy and playing guitar.
• Onder used to play in a band.
