## [employee-description]
### Guillermo Barbero
Technical Lead • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Guillermo Barbero Andreu is highly motivated by teamwork and by being surrounded by talented professionals who demonstrate strong human values. He thrives in environments where continuous learning happens naturally through collaboration and shared experiences.
‍
He enjoys working in multicultural settings and values the opportunity to collaborate with people who bring diverse perspectives to the challenges that projects present. Guillermo is particularly interested in the overall management of projects, with a strong desire to be involved throughout all phases and maintain a broad understanding of the entire delivery process from start to finish.
Driven by continuous improvement, he enjoys developing new ideas and approaches, challenging conventional ways of working, and identifying smarter, more efficient solutions that add value to projects and teams alike.
Backstory and Achievements
Throughout his career, Guillermo Barbero Andreu has been involved in major international construction and infrastructure projects in highly demanding environments. He has gained extensive experience across planning, project controls, construction delivery, and multidisciplinary coordination.
Among the most significant projects he has contributed to are the Riyadh Metro, one of the world's largest urban transport developments, the redevelopment of Santiago Bernabéu Stadium for Real Madrid CF, and the development of a hyperscale data center campus delivered within a complex EPC environment. These experiences have enabled him to build a strong understanding of large-scale project delivery and the successful coordination of diverse stakeholders across all phases of construction.
These projects enabled Guillermo Barbero Andreu to develop strong expertise in integrated project planning, project controls, construction sequencing, stakeholder coordination, subcontractor and contract management, and the integration of critical infrastructure, including 66/20 kV electrical substations, into overall construction strategies.
Working on fast-paced international projects with demanding schedules and multiple interfaces has strengthened both his technical expertise and organizational capabilities. These experiences have equipped him with the ability to manage complexity, coordinate diverse stakeholders, and contribute effectively to the successful delivery of large-scale construction and infrastructure projects.
Guillermo Barbero Andreu considers himself a proactive, creative, and results-oriented professional who enjoys working as part of a team. He has a particular interest in project controls and detailed organization, taking pride in bringing structure, clarity, and effective coordination to complex situations.
His collaborative approach, combined with a strong focus on planning and execution, enables him to contribute positively to project teams while supporting the successful delivery of project objectives.
Fun Facts
Outside of work, Guillermo Barbero Andreu has a strong interest in water sports, football, and Padel. He enjoys staying active and values the teamwork, discipline, and competitiveness that these activities promote.
He is also passionate about cooking, regularly attending cooking courses and spending time preparing meals at home. For Guillermo, cooking is both a creative outlet and an opportunity to continuously learn and explore new cultures and cuisines.
