## [employee-description]
### Micael Agostinho
Fulfilment and Leadership Coach • People Operations
What Drives Him
Micael carries out his activity with passion and enormous engagement. He starts from the premise that there is evidence that behavior and emotions are changeable and that anyone can change their experience in favor of a better quality of life and emotional well-being. He is very dedicated and persistent and recognizes that each person has their own developmental path. He believes that his practice involves the components of science, technique and art.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Born in France, he completed most of his path in Portugal, where he graduated in Psychology and obtained a research grant from the Foundation for Science and Technology (FCT). He has worked as a professor in university, as a psychologist in social solidarity institutions and in private clinical practice since 2005.
Formal Qualifications
• Degree in Psychology (2005)
• PhD in Clinical Psychology (2012)
Fun Facts
• Learned to play guitar by himself at age 13
• He is a former 400m and 800m athlete who now practices running as a hobby.
