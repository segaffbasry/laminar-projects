## [employee-description]
### Kevin Miralles
Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Mark Kevin is driven by challenges and sees them as opportunities for growth, innovation, and continuous improvement. He is passionate about finding practical and innovative solutions that help deliver successful projects and create lasting value. He values collaboration, enjoys learning from diverse experiences and is equally passionate about mentoring and sharing knowledge with the next generation of professionals.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Mark Kevin started his professional journey with one of the leading construction companies in the Philippines, where he began as a Cadet Engineer a and steadily progressing into senior planning roles before eventually leading the Planning Department. In addition to his contractor side experience, he has also worked for internationally based companies.
With years of industry experience, he has developed strong expertise in construction planning, scheduling and project controls for large-scale developments across the Philippines, Australia, and Taiwan. His project experience spans mixed-use developments, high-rise residential buildings, land development works, office buildings, hotels and chemical storage terminal facilities.
Throughout his career, he has been involved in project planning and monitoring, schedule development, stakeholder coordination, and supporting teams in achieving efficient and timely project delivery.
Formal Qualifications
• Licensed Civil Engineer
• Licensed Master Plumber
• Accredited Materials Engineer - I
• Completed academic units for Master of Science in Construction Management
Fun Facts
Outside of work, Mark Kevin enjoys exploring new destinations and gaining insights from different cultures and experiences. He is passionate about camping and spending time in nature and he also enjoys watching anime series and reading manga.
