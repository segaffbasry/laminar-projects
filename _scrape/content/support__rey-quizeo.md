### Rey Quizeo
People Operations Specialist • People Operations
What Drives Him
Rey believes that his passion and dedication to creating an environment for everyone to flourish makes him unique. It is about going above and beyond the role and satisfying the needs of the key stakeholders and partners. He is motivated by building sustainable relationships and making a difference not just to the people we work with but to the environment and community we live in. His desire is to help the community and be part of the great movement in imparting change to the triple bottom line – Planet, People, and Profit. Lastly, be a driving force for diversity, equity, and inclusion.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Rey is an agile and experienced Human Resource Professional with over Seven years of experience in the corporate industry. In his pursuit of knowledge in the field of business and management, he pursued his Master’s degree in Business Administration. His greatest accomplishment was the Great Place to Work project and ISO 9001:2015 certification. He was able to lead these major projects and got the certifications for his previous companies.
Formal Qualifications
• Master’s in Business Administration (ongoing)
• Registered Psychometrician (RPm)
• BA Psychology – Cum Laude
Fun Facts
• Loves to sing and perform. Jhonrey has joined local and national choral competitions and vocal workshops.
