### Streamlining Project Execution for a Hyperscale Data Centre in Belgium

## [main-body]
Laminar Projects was tasked with orchestrating the critical MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing) installation and commissioning phases of a hyperscale data centre for a global tech leader in Belgium. Our mandate was to address significant programmatic and logistical challenges and drive the project to completion within a constrained timeframe and resource setting.
: A major international technology company
Client
: 9 Months
Project Duration
: Construction and Commissioning of a Hyperscale Data Centre
Project Scope
‍
### The Challenge
The project was hampered by a series of complexities:
1.: The existing project schedule was fragmented, with considerable scope overlooked and sequences misaligned.
Incomplete Program
2. : Numerous contractors were involved, with low-constructability design intensifying interface management challenges.
Contractor Coordination
3.: The absence of a cohesive plan led to disarray in site operations and supply chain management.
Integrated Planning Deficiency
4. : Progress reporting mechanisms were non-existent, clouding project visibility.
Reporting Void
5.: A gap in tracking and understanding delays impeded effective prioritization and mitigation efforts.
Delay Diagnosis
### Our Approach
In alignment with Laminar Projects' distinct capabilities for rigorous and authentic engagement, our approach encompassed:
- : We developed a comprehensive construction and commissioning program that became the cornerstone for prioritizing activities.
Reconstructing the Execution Plan
- ‍: Transitioning from discipline-centric packages to area-focused management, we equipped managers with weekly action items, enhancing on-site productivity and issue resolution.
Emphasis Shift to Area Management
- : Our use of automated reporting and dashboards for key metrics such as labor and cost provided a transparent and factual basis for decisions.
Data-Driven Decision Making
### Outputs Delivered
- : A meticulously detailed blueprint to manage the interplay between various trades.
MEP Construction Programme
- ‍: A robust schedule integrated with construction activities, underpinned by commissioning sequences.
Commissioning Programme
- : Tactical lookahead schedules fostered by area-specific workshops.
Weekly Operational Forecasts
- : Advanced dashboards and tagging trackers for real-time commissioning updates.
Progress Tracking Tools
### Key Outcomes
- : Our information was a bedrock of accuracy, optimizing effective work time utilization.
Reliability
- ‍: Despite daily fluctuations, our strategic planning ensured that team efforts were aligned with long-term objectives.
Directional Clarity
- : We brought an infectious zeal to the project, personalizing delivery and energizing teams.
Inspirational Leadership
### Conclusion
Our engagement with the hyperscale data centre project in Belgium exemplifies Laminar Projects' philosophy: that a successful program is not just a reporting instrument but a dynamic tool that actively propels on-site execution. By fostering construction team buy-in and embedding best practices, we ensure that strategic planning translates into tangible on-ground action.
