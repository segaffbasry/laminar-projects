## [employee-description]
### Ali Kaan Yildiz
Senior Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
As a human being, nothing makes Ali Kaan happier than helping others. He believes that acts of kindness are the key to becoming a better person. He consistently strives to bring positive energy and a warm smile to those around him.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Born and raised in Turkey, Ali Kaan left his home at a very young age for the opportunity to attend a Science and Technology High School. Later, he graduated with a degree in Civil Engineering from Istanbul Technical University.
He has since pursued a career in project controls with leading Turkish contractors in Kuwait and Tanzania. Subsequently, he joined the leading EV battery manufacturer Northvolt in Sweden as a project planner, where he had the opportunity to work in a diverse environment.
Formal Qualifications
• Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering
• Proficient in Primavera P6 and Microsoft Project
• Certified in FIDIC Contracts and Claims Management
Fun Facts
• Ali Kaan speaks English, Turkish, and hopefully soon Spanish.
• He is a huge basketball fan, and his favorite player is Vince Carter.
• He loves traveling, gaming, and story writing.
