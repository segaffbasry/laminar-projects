## [op6---faqs]
Frequently asked questions about Oracle Aconex
What is Oracle Aconex used for?
Aconex is a construction management platform used to manage drawings, RFIs, approvals, communications and more — all in one system. It’s designed for large, complex projects where tracking changes, decisions, and documents is essential.
How is Aconex different from other document management software?
Aconex is built for construction projects. It has structured workflows, full audit trails, and mobile access, so it keeps pace with live project delivery. It’s not just for storing files — it helps project teams stay aligned and accountable.
How can Laminar help with setup and licensing?
As certified Oracle solutions engineer specialists and accredited resellers, we handle procurement, licensing, rollout, and ongoing support, working from inside your team to make Aconex part of your actual delivery process.
