## [employee-description]
### Pooranan Yogarajah
Senior Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Pooranan is deeply motivated by personal growth, continuous learning, and his development as a professional and human being. He's driven by his profession's role in advancing a sustainable future, understanding that technological advancements in construction are crucial for achieving global sustainability goals. Success and client satisfaction further ignite his passion, as he takes pride in delivering impactful results that elevate his professional accomplishments and contribute to the broader mission of building a better world.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Originally from Sri Lanka, Pooranan began his career as a civil engineer before transitioning into planning and project control. After moving to the UK to pursue a master’s in construction management, he accumulated experience working on various infrastructure projects, ranging from highways to high-tech buildings.
His expertise goes beyond technical knowledge — Pooranan’s ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams and engage with stakeholders has been instrumental in his success. Through his commitment to delivering complex projects efficiently, he plays a vital role in driving innovation and excellence in every project he undertakes.
Formal Qualifications
• MEng in Civil and Construction Engineering
• MSc in Construction Project Management
Fun Facts
• Pooranan loves playing cricket. He's a top-order batsman in a cricket club in Bristol.
• He also enjoys watching movies from around the world.
