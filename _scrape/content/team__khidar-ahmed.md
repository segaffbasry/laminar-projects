## [employee-description]
### Khidar Ahmed
Delivery Lead • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Khidar is a dedicated and proactive project manager with a strong background in the design and construction phases of projects. He's passionate about delivering projects on time, within budget, and exceeding quality expectations. With his creative and analytical mindset, Khidar thinks beyond boundaries and strives for innovative solutions. He's committed to data-driven decision-making and leveraging all available tools to drive project delivery.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Originally from India, Khidar has gained valuable experience in the construction industry in both the UK and the UAE. He has worked as a project manager in London, managing multiple commercial housing and super luxury residential projects. His leadership and organisational skills contributed to a significant increase in client satisfaction through the adoption of cloud-based project management software.‍
Prior to his experience in the UK, Khidar worked on large-scale commercial and real estate projects in Dubai valued at around £100 million. Khidar demonstrated exceptional project planning and coordination skills, resulting in activities being consistently ahead of schedule. He effectively managed multiple teams and maintained strict quality control standards throughout all project activities. With a strong focus on safety, Khidar ensured zero accident events by implementing comprehensive safety protocols and fostering a culture of health, safety, and environmental awareness.
Formal Qualifications
• MSc (Eng) International Construction Management and Engineering
• Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM®)
• Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering
Fun Facts
• Khidar enjoys cooking and trying out different cuisines.
• He's also passionate about road trips and travelling and finds solace in nature, especially beaches.
