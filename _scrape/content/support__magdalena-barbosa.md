## [employee-description]
### Magdalena Barbosa
Talent Acquisition Director • People Operations
What Drives Her
Magdalena is passionate about people and firmly believes they are the cornerstone of any successful team. She plays a vital role in placing individuals where they can thrive, develop, and feel valued. An eternal optimist, Magdalena adapts creatively when faced with challenges. She takes pride in her exceptional interpersonal skills, which enable her to build meaningful relationships and understand individuals and their motivations deeply.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Originally from Poland, Magdalena has spent a significant portion of her life working and living in the UK before eventually settling in Portugal. With over a decade of experience in talent acquisition, she's gained extensive expertise in global recruitment and talent management. Her corporate career spans various industries, including retail, chemical, and automotive manufacturing. Now, she's dedicated to transforming the recruitment world for the better.
Formal Qualifications
• BA (Hons) in Business Management
• HND in Business Economics
Fun Facts
• Magdalena is passionate about baking and is confident that her red fruit cheesecake is among the best you'll ever taste.
• She loves snow more than sun and regularly visits the mountains in winter.
• She is fluent in three languages: Polish, English, and Portuguese.
