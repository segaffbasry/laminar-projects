## [employee-description]
### Rory Kalnins
Support Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Rory is motivated by working collaboratively with others to achieve a common goal and deliver successful project outcomes. He enjoys solving problems, finding practical solutions to challenges, and contributing to the smooth delivery of projects to a high standard.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Originally from Australia, Rory began his career in the commercial construction industry, contributing to the delivery of hospitality, education, and cultural & civic infrastructure projects across regional New South Wales. These experiences have provided him with a strong foundation in project delivery and stakeholder collaboration. He is proud of the relationships he has built throughout his career and of consistently delivering high-quality outcomes as part of diverse project teams.
Formal Qualifications
• Bachelor of Construction Management (Building) with First Class Honours.
Fun Facts
• Keen sportsman who has played competitive cricket, football, and Australian Rules Football (AFL).
• Enjoys reading, running, exploring the outdoors, experiencing new things and travelling.
• Values teamwork, leadership, and continuous learning both on and off the field.
