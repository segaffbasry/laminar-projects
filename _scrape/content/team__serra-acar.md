## [employee-description]
### Serra Acar
Senior Consultant • Project Management Consulting
### er
What Drives H
First as a human, then as a civil engineer, Serra is driven by a desire to positively impact communities and the environment. She loves paying attention to detail and precision. She believes communication is essential to solving problems and that asking the right questions paves the way for decision-makers.‍
Serra is a civil engineer who believes learning never stops. Supporting her clients with new materials/technologies, techniques, and regulations provides her with opportunities for ongoing education and personal growth.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Originally from Turkey, Serra now lives in Istanbul.‍
She has worked with a contractor, Yapı Merkezi, on different projects in Tanzania and Ethiopia. She expanded her knowledge regarding project management, planning and cost control. Serra later decided to pursue her career at Mott MacDonald as a project manager, where she worked with cross-functional teams, and communicated effectively with stakeholders worldwide.
Formal Qualifications
• MSc in Construction Management
• Bachelor of Civil Engineering
Fun Facts
• Serra enjoys travelling, running and pilates.
• She loves sailing into the blue and soaking up the sun.
