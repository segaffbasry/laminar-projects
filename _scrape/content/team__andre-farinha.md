## [employee-description]
### Andre Farinha
Delivery Lead • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
André thrives on turning complex engineering problems into efficient solutions. He is driven by a deep passion for transforming complex engineering challenges into practical, efficient, and innovative solutions. His motivation lies in streamlining processes, enhancing safety, boosting quality, and promoting smarter and more sustainable construction practices. In collaborative environments, he excels at bringing clarity and structure to dynamic projects, always focused on delivering high-value results from concept to completion. This constant drive to build better fuels his enthusiasm for every new challenge.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Originally from Portugal, André completed his Master’s degree in Structural Engineering at NOVA University of Lisbon in 2012. Driven by a desire to grow and expand his skills, he moved to London in 2014 to take on a new challenge in his career. Since then, he has contributed to a wide range of key and complex engineering projects within the enabling and structural sectors, including demolition, structural alterations & retention, deep basements, piling works, groundworks, substructures and superstructures.
André has led planning and technical management across both pre-construction and construction phases of major London developments. He has collaborated with multidisciplinary teams to ensure technical excellence and seamless execution. His achievements include developing innovative methodologies and construction solutions to address complex structural challenges, as well as designing robust logistics and phasing strategies to keep high-stakes projects on track and aligned with client expectations.
Formal Qualifications
• MSc in Structural Engineering
• A61 AP (NVQ Level 6)
• CITB SMSTS
• CITB TWC
• CSCS Card (Qualified Professional)
Fun Facts
• An avid traveler, André loves exploring new places and cultures – especially if it means trying all the local food. He’s always keen to connect with locals, hear their stories, and learn what makes each place unique.
• He’s a passionate football fan who loves a kickabout with friends and colleagues whenever he gets the chance.
• He enjoys unwinding with a great film or a binge-worthy TV series.
• Most importantly, André is the proud dad of a two-year-old and a newborn daughter – a pair who already keep him on his toes and smiling every day.
