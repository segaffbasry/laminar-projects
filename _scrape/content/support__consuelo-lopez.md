## [employee-description]
### Consuelo López
Business Operations Specialist • Operations
What Drives Her
Consuelo is driven by a deep commitment to people – making sure customers feel heard, supported, and genuinely cared for. She finds energy in solving problems, refining processes, and catching the small things that make a big difference. But she doesn’t do it alone – she thrives in collaboration, working closely with teams across the business to connect the dots, close gaps, and keep things running with heart and precision.
‍
What motivates her most is knowing that her work creates real, positive impact for both customers and her team.
Backstory and Achievements
He was born in Venezuela, where he studied Industrial Organization Engineering. With family from Colombia and Spain, she grew up with a mix of cultures.
She moved alone from Venezuela to Spain, where she learned and played the role of a bidder in the architecture and engineering, pharmaceutical and medical device industries.
Not having moved around enough in her life, she decided to move to live in England with very basic language skills; her first job was as a waitress at Pret a Manger and from there she built a career path in client/partner account management roles particularly in the channel incentives sector, where she worked with blue-chip clients in the technology industry.
Latest achievement: She got her dream job working for Laminar, a company with a dynamic, flexible, multicultural and people-focused mindset and approach. Here the new story starts...
