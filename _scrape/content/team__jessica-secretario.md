## [employee-description]
### Jessica Secretario
Senior Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Her
Jessica is fueled by her desire in helping build things that benefit her community. She is passionate in finding optimal solutions to problems and likes to create efficient ways of doing things. She finds joy in learning and considers her self a forever student of life. She believes in the power of collaborative effort and team work as she is inspired to share her knowledge and skills to other people.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Raised in the Philippines, Jessica started her engineering career in the construction industry as a planner. She was privileged to be part of huge construction firms that execute major government projects in Manila. She handled multiple infrastructure projects, particularly railway and elevated highway construction. She has also led training programs for planning and project management for novice and aspiring planners in her previous organizations.
Formal Qualifications
She finished her degree of BS in Industrial Engineering and acquired certifications in Primavera P6 and TILOS Linear Scheduling software for construction projects.
Fun Facts
Jessica is a jump rope enthusiast and loves doing jump rope exercises and tricks as away of keeping an active lifestyle. As a food-lover and adventure-seeker, she also loves travelling to new places and experiencing different cultures.
