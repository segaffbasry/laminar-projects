## [employee-description]
### Luke Burgess
Associate Director • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Luke is driven by delivery, maintaining high quality projects and building relationships whilst streamlining the process for all parties, with a keen interest in using new technology to aid this. His aim is to provide a solution for complex projects simplifying for all parties through the full lifecycle of the project.
‍
Luke enjoys highly complex engineering/temporary works and logistical issues and the challenges this provides.
Backstory and Achievements
From Durham and worked and studied across the UK in a variety of sectors.
Luke has predominantly worked as a construction manager and senior project engineer with Main Contractors and Subcontractors in Highways, Rail, Heritage, Building, Preconstruction, Design & planning sectors in both the public and private sectors. The project’s values range from £1 – 500 million with my day-to-day role involving being the key person in charge of lifting operations, temporary works, QA/QC, Service management, and Digitisation.
Formal Qualifications
• BEng Hons Civil & structural engineering
• CPCS – Appointed person – Lifting operations
• CSCS – SMSTS
Fun Facts
• Played rugby for many years, avid Newcastle united fan, and currently learning to surf.
• A massive foodie who is an avid cook, enjoys keeping fit, being outdoors.
• The rest of my spare time is spent traveling and renovating the house and garden.
