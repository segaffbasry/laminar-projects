## [op6---faqs]
Frequently asked questions about Oracle Primavera Unifier
What is Primavera Unifier used for?
Unifier is a project controls and lifecycle management platform, used to manage budgets, contracts, documents, and workflows — all in one system. It gives project teams better visibility and auditability across complex capital programmes.
What is the difference between Primavera P6 and Primavera Unifier?
Primavera P6 is for scheduling and progress tracking. Unifier is for managing cost, contracts, approvals, and documentation. Many teams use both — with P6 driving time and dependencies, and Unifier covering the commercial and governance side.
How does Laminar help with licensing and setup?
We’re certified Oracle solutions engineer specialists and accredited resellers — so we can handle procurement, licensing, configuration, and training. We don’t just turn the system on; we make sure it’s embedded in how your team actually delivers.
