## [employee-description]
### Tino John
Delivery Lead • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Tino thrives on progress — in every project, every interaction, and every challenge. Whether it's advancing within the projects he's working on, fostering meaningful connections with colleagues, or seeing personal growth in himself and others, Tino is fueled by the momentum of continuous improvement. His passion for learning is unmatched; he's always eager to acquire new skills, broaden his knowledge, and further his development daily.
‍
A true team player, Tino is highly collaborative and enjoys working in dynamic environments where teamwork is key. He believes that success is best achieved when shared, and he values the connections and synergy that come from working with others.
Backstory and Achievements
Tino's journey began in Germany, where he grew up and studied Automotive Engineering. Soon after, he ventured abroad to Austria to kick-start his career in the automotive industry — a move that led to him settling in the beautiful Austrian landscape, which he enjoys on a daily basis.
It didn’t take long for Tino to discover his true passion for management. His early career saw him take on roles in project and business management, where he quickly developed a keen eye for leadership and strategic direction. Now, at Laminar, Tino is diving into the construction sector, marking a new chapter in his career.
He's excited to contribute his expertise while expanding his skills and knowledge in this new field.
Formal Qualifications
• Automotive Engineering Degree
Fun Facts
• Tino is a bundle of positive energy and enthusiasm!
• He's always excited about trying new things, whether it's exploring new sports (perhaps explaining his vast array of hobbies…), traveling to new places, or tasting exotic cuisines. His curiosity knows no bounds, and he approaches every new adventure with a smile.
