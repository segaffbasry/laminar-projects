## [employee-description]
### Aung Sint
Technical Lead • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Customer satisfaction is something that keeps Aung going. After having completed many projects, it clears to him that delivering the values that meet client’s needs is what ultimately determines the project success. Aung believes that active involvement from all project stakeholders as well as effective communication among them are critical to project success.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Originally from Myanmar (Burma), Aung started his project management career in Singapore, mostly as a main contractor.
With more than 13 years of experience in Singapore construction industry, he’s handled different types of projects ranging from residential and mixed-development commercial to infrastructure and industrial, involving in every stage of project life cycle.
Formal Qualifications
• Master of Business Administration
• Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering
• Bachelor of Arts in Mathematics and Physics
• Certified Program Management Professional (PgMP®)
• Certified Project Management Professional (PMP®)
• PMI Agile Certified Practitioner (PMI-ACP®)
• PMI Risk Management Professional (PMI-RMP®)
Fun Facts
• Speaks Burmese and English.
• Loves to travel around the world.
• Practice meditation and study Buddha’s teachings.
