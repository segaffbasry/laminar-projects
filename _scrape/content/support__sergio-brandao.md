## [employee-description]
### Sérgio Brandão
IT Specialist • Operations
What Drives Him
He has a knack for diving into new technologies and figuring out how they can be used to make things better. In the IT world, where he's right at home, he gets a real kick out of solving problems and fixing what's broken. It's like a puzzle for him, and he loves the challenge of putting the pieces together. Whether it's a tricky bug or a complex system issue, he's in his element when he's finding solutions and helping others understand and use the tools they need. It's this mix of curiosity, problem-solving, and teamwork that makes his job so satisfying.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Originally from Porto, Portugal, Sérgio has also spent part of his life in Lisbon. Influenced by his older friends; many of whom are now software developers, he developed an early interest in computers and became a daily Linux user for several years. Although he initially pursued a career in translation and language teaching, Sérgio eventually decided to return to his roots and transitioned to a role as a systems administrator in the software development industry. One of his most memorable achievements was accidentally disconnecting an entire office of over 100 people from the internet and then quickly restoring the connection before anyone could get upset with him.
Formal Qualifications
• Google certified as an IT Support, System Administration and IT Infrastructure Services Specialist
• Currently pursuing a Microsoft Cybersecurity certification
Fun Facts
• Speaks Portuguese, English, a bit of French and basic Hindi.
• Sergio loves to read, the Bible being his favorite book, but he also enjoys reading about time management and productivity.
• He once bought a car that had over 900.000 kms and drove it past 1 million himself.
• Taught himself how to code. Made it in a couple months. Tried to teach himself how to play the guitar. It’s been 15 years, and he still can’t do it.
