## [employee-description]
### Emre Gokyigit
Senior Associate Director • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Emre is passionate about delivering the project needs with the highest degree of efficiency and accuracy. To achieve that, he combines analytical skills with a sensitivity for aesthetics to create visual project management tools and reports. Supporting his team members with expertise and creating a positive work environment are his top motivations.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Originally from Turkey, Emre has also lived and worked in Algeria and Qatar.
He has previously worked for Alstom and Yapi Merkezi. He's been a senior planning engineer, senior project controls engineer and project risk manager in railway projects ranging from £200 million to £2 billion. He joined Laminar in 2021 and has supported our clients with his expertise ever since.
His performance has been recognised both by the independent auditors and his clients. He's a detail-oriented problem-solver with broad and transferable competencies in schedule, performance, risk and commercial management.
Formal Qualifications
• BSc in Civil Engineering
• MSc in Construction Management
• Project Management Professional (PMP)
• Membership of the Association for Project Management (MAPM)
Fun Facts
• Emre speaks Turkish, English and French.
• He considers travelling as shooting an imaginary documentary about the culture and history of the places he visits. He's been to more than 30 countries.
• He's a CMAS Two-Star scuba diver – because travelling under the water is even more interesting.
• Emre loves urban photography, especially taking bridge photos.
• He's enthusiastic to learn about etymology and the links between different languages.
