## [employee-description]
### Aga Domingo
Senior Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Aga is motivated and inspired by the love of his family. To him, the family drives him to put his best foot forward in all things and take a God-centred approach to everything. In addition, knowing that project planning and controls significantly impact projects motivates him to be better at what he’s working on.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Aga has six years of experience in project planning and controls for horizontal and vertical building projects. He has also handled multiple projects as a planning supervisor and overseeing a team of planners for a whole estate of projects.
Formal Qualifications
• Master of Science in Construction Management (Ongoing)
• Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering
Fun Facts
• Loves cooking and also do baking.
• A certified coffee addict.
• Likes to do almost everything.
