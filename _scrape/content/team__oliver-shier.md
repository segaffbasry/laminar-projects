## [employee-description]
### Oliver Shier
Associate Director • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Oliver is driven by his desire to help others and finds forming a collaborative environment is the best way to make progress efficiently. He has worked in some fantastic teams where he has been encouraged to adapt, develop, take accountability and always make decisions based on the best outcome for the project and community. He takes great pride in trying to relay the same message as a mentor and colleague to those around him.‍
He likes to test himself to see how he adapts to new situations and is always eager to pick-up new skills or experiences. He thinks differences in a team are a huge strength, and it is essential to create working environments where unique concepts can be put forward, explored and implemented where suitable.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Oliver grew up on the small island of Guernsey. At a young age, he used to areas of Africa with his grandparents’ charity.‍
The first construction project he worked on involved planning a major railway upgrade in Malaysia during a university year in industry. Here, he learned how different upbringings and life experiences helped generate complementary thoughts and ideas.‍
Since then, he has worked for a major contractor on several different construction projects within the UK, operating under planning, engineering and management roles. He is very grateful for this experience which enabled him to become a member of the Institution of Civil Engineers.Oliver has now moved to France so that his wife could be closer to her family and is relishing the new challenge.
Formal Qualifications
• MEng
• CEng MICE – ICE
Fun Facts
• Oliver likes adventure and frequently attempts challenges such as distance runs or mountain hikes.
• He enjoys playing almost any sport, though has a few favourites in tennis, football and badminton.
• He has a two-year-old Jack Russell with endless energy who is hard to keep up with.
