## [employee-description]
### Ali Cobanbeyli
Technical Lead • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Ali Cuneyt Cobanbeyli is passionate about planning, optimizing, and streamlining project processes. He aims to achieve success in time and resource management by bringing a strategic perspective to projects through various tools and techniques. His drive comes from a constant desire to learn and solve challenges, which keeps him motivated in his professional journey.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Ali Cuneyt Cobanbeyli has over a decade of international experience in the construction industry, working on projects totaling 4.3 billion Euros. He has contributed to achieving project goals by balancing time, resources, and cost management in the projects he has worked on. His efficiency in planning and project control processes has been a key strength in driving project success. In addition, he has supported commercial processes, offering a well-rounded contribution to the projects.
Formal Qualifications
• BSc in Architecture (Istanbul Bilgi University)
• Proficient in SAP, Primavera P6, AutoCAD, Revit, and cost control tools
• Skilled in Visual Basic and Python (beginner level); currently learning Dynamo and Power BI
• Advanced Excel and Primavera P6 expertise for data analysis and projectplanning
Fun Facts
• Ali loves discovering new countries and exploring their architectural values.
• He enjoys 3D modeling and spends time creating videos as a hobby.
• He is a big fan of camping and fishing.
