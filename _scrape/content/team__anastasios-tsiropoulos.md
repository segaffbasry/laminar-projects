## [employee-description]
### Anastasis Tsiropoulos
Senior Consultant • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Anastasis is motivated by continuous development and improvement. He enjoys learning new skills and growing both personally and professionally. Always curious, he actively seeks new solutions to challenges and believes there is always room for improvement. Anastasis is also an advocate for teamwork and firmly believes that the whole is greater than the sum of its parts.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Born and raised in Thessaloniki, Greece, Anastasis earned a full scholarship to pursue his Master’s degree in Project Management at the University of Sussex. He moved to Brighton, UK, in 2018 and, following his studies, began his career in project controls. He has worked at both contractor and consultancy level as a Project Planner and Reporting Analyst, contributing to projects across a range of industries.
Formal Qualifications
• MSc in Environmental Protection and Sustainable Development
• MSc in Project Management
• APM – Project Management Qualification
Fun Facts
• Anastasis goes on hiking trips every year with his friends… mostly due to FOMO.
• He loves cooking, and his retirement dream is to run a small breakfast shop on a Greek island.
• He enjoys travelling and learning about new cultures and civilizations.
