## [employee-description]
### Diaa Khalaf
Technical Lead • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Diaa is driven by a passion for innovation and excellence in the field of construction management. His motivation stems from the desire to bridge the gap between traditional construction practices and cutting-edge technologies. Diaa is deeply committed to leveraging technology to streamline project controls, enhance efficiency, and reduce costs while maintaining the highest safety and quality standards. His drive is further fueled by the challenges and opportunities to lead diverse teams towards achieving shared goals and completing projects that leave a lasting impact on communities.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Diaa embarked on his journey in the construction industry with a civil engineering degree from Mansoura University. His career is marked by a series of progressive roles, from a civil site engineer to a project controls manager. Diaa has over 14 years of experience managing and controlling large-scale construction projects, demonstrating exceptional skills in cost management, team leadership, and client relations.
Formal Qualifications
• Master of Science in Construction Project Management with (BIM)
• Master of Science in Civil Engineering
• Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering
• Project Management Professional (PMP)
• PMI Risk Management Professional (PMI-RMP)
Fun Facts
• Diaa bears a profound appreciation for the journey as much as the destination, particularly enjoying the freedom and flexibility that come with travelling by car.
• This preference for road trips reflects his love for exploring the landscapes, cultures, and nuances of different places at his own pace.
• The experience of driving through varying topography, from bustling city streets to serene countryside roads, offers him a unique perspective on the world.
