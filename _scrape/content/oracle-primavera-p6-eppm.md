## [op6---faqs]
Frequently asked questions about Oracle Primavera P6
What is Primavera P6 EPPM used for?
Primavera P6 EPPM (Enterprise Project Portfolio Management) is Oracle’s flagship project and portfolio management solution. It's widely used across the construction and engineering industries for planning, progress tracking, and controlling especially complex projects. It helps teams stay on top of timelines, budgets, and progress by providing a structured way to manage activities, resources, and reporting across multiple projects.
What’s the difference between Primavera P6 Professional and P6 EPPM?
Primavera P6 Professional is a desktop application, typically used by planners and schedulers working on individual project files. P6 EPPM is the web-based, enterprise-grade version — specifically designed for managing schedules, costs, and data across a portfolio of projects, with shared access, dashboards, and real-time reporting.
How does Laminar help teams get more from Primavera P6?
We don’t just install P6 — we embed it into the way your team actually delivers. That means structuring schedules properly, linking them to progress and cost, and setting up dashboards that make sense. We’re certified solutions engineer specialists and accredited Oracle resellers, so we can handle everything from licensing to rollout.
Does Laminar offer Primavera P6 training?
Yes! Our P6 training is designed to build both individual and team capability through a structured and practical approach. We combine planning theory with hands-on application using P6 tools, reinforced through practice exercises and personalised one-to-one feedback.Training is delivered through weekly online or in-person sessions, with exercises set between sessions to help embed learning. A final course assessment ensures key concepts are understood and retained.We’re flexible in how your training is delivered — whether you prefer a condensed, intensive format or a deeper dive into specific topics, we’ll tailor the sessions to suit your needs and priorities. Our training leader also provides support throughout, including feedback on live project commitments where relevant.If you're interested in developing your P6 capability, get in touch — we’d be happy to discuss how we can support your team.
