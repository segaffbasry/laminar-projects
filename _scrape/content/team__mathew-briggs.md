## [employee-description]
### Mathew Briggs
Principal • Project Management Consulting
What Drives Him
Mathew (yes that’s with one “t”) is our guru for all things data analytics. He combines his passion for technology and data with his domain knowledge as a civil engineer and project planner to create extremely useful tools for our clients to make data driven decisions. Mat is not a technological extremist and is fully aware the people come first, technology just enables them to do their jobs better when implemented properly.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Originally from the UK he has a background in main contracting and subcontracting where he has worked as a project planner on projects between £1m and £1B. His most formative experience was spending a number of years as a project planner for Costain on the London Bridge Station project where he learned about the realities of delivering £1B projects with a multitude of public interfaces.
While working in industry he came across the field of data science and analytics and made himself an expert using every online resource he could find. Now he is an industry leader in the field delivering implementation of the latest technologies he can find a practical use for in a project context.
Formal Qualifications
• Bachelor of Civil Engineering
• Institute of Civil Engineers (ICE)
Fun Facts
• Speaks English and Japanese.
• Enjoys climbing, technology and data.
