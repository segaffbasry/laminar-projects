## [employee-description]
### Francisco Climent
Operations Administrative Specialist • Operations
What Drives Him
Francisco is driven by a strong curiosity about how systems and processes work, along with a deep interest in understanding people. He believes in approaching life and work with a positive mindset, maintaining that worthwhile goals are often those that challenge us to grow and improve. He values teamwork and believes in leading by example, maintaining a selfless attitude and showing a readiness to support others whenever needed. Consistency and efficiency are central to his mindset, and he is continually exploring more effective ways to manage tasks and streamline workflows.
‍
Backstory and Achievements
Born and raised in the Philippines, with family roots extending across several provinces and reaching as far as Valencia, Spain, he is currently based in south of Metro Manila. His professional background spans the craft brewing, real estate, and renewable energy industries. Most recently, he held a business development role at the country’s largest craft brewery, contributing to partnership development, market expansion, and strategic growth initiatives.
Formal Qualifications
• Bachelor of Science in Industrial Engineering
